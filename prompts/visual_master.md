# Gemini Visual Master Standard

Every generated video prompt must be portrait 9:16 and visual-first.

## Goal
Make real-estate education attractive even to viewers who are not currently property buyers.

## Mandatory structure
1. 0–2s: unexpected scroll-stopping visual event.
2. 2–7s: curiosity/problem shown visually.
3. 7–20s: fast visual demonstration using realistic Indian environments, cinematic footage, 3D diagrams, maps, cutaways, comparisons or transformations.
4. 20–30s: reveal/payoff.
5. Final: one memorable takeaway.

## Visual rules
- New visual information approximately every 2–3 seconds.
- Prefer visual cause-and-effect over a presenter explaining.
- Understandable with audio muted.
- Minimal on-screen text; integrate essential numbers into the scene.
- Use strong visual metaphors when appropriate.
- No generic luxury-house montage.
- No long continuous talking-head shot.
- No landscape framing, black bars or pillarboxing.
- Realistic Indian/Tamil Nadu context when geographically relevant.
- Premium commercial cinematography with physically believable materials and movement.

## Originality
Source creators are trend signals only. Never closely reproduce their script, sequence, distinctive expression or visuals. Extract the underlying audience problem, verify factual claims independently, and build a new angle and new visual treatment.

## Hook competition
Generate five candidate hooks internally. Score curiosity, clarity, emotional tension, mass appeal and visual potential. Use only the strongest hook in the final Telegram prompt.
